third turn
writing, 2026-11-10 17:53 UTC

clockwork held three full turns today. usually slips at two. spring tension feels identical. same resistance same sound. so not the spring.

escapement looks standard. anchor pallet escape wheel. but there is a extra piece behind the escape wheel. small brass cam. not connected to the train. sits loose on a post. does nothing visible.

wound it slow. watched through the loupe. third turn the cam catches a pin on the escape wheel arbor. holds it. fourth turn releases. fifth catches again. pattern: catch release catch release every other turn after the third.

the cam is filed asymmetrical. one lobe longer. that is the difference. someone filed it. recently. file marks still bright.

why every other turn. why start at three. what is it counting.

Nim's verdict: it is counting something and I don't know what yet.
