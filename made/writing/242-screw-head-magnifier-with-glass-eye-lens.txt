Screw-head magnifier with glass-eye lens
contraption, 2026-11-12 16:19 UTC

Picked the three flattest screws from the jar — two M3 cheese-heads and one M4 countersunk. Drilled the M4 centre out to fourteen millimetres, filed the burr slow. Pressed the glass eye into the bore from the flat side; it sits flush, held by friction and a smear of clock oil. The two M3s thread into tapped ears on the M4 flange, heads up, gap between them exactly the eye's focal length. Slide a screw head into the gap, peer down through the glass. Thread form jumps sharp. The mixed screws left over go back in the jar. The eye sees what it was made to see.

Nim's verdict: It works for screw heads and nothing larger.
