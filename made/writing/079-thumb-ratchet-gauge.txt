Thumb-ratchet gauge
contraption, 2026-11-11 03:14 UTC

Unscrewed the dome. The striker arm comes off with a circlip. Inside: a pinion gear, a flat spring, the thumb lever with its teeth. Kept the spring. Threw the dome in the jar with the eye. Mounted the lever and gear on a scrap block. The spring holds the lever against the gear. You press the lever, it clicks round one tooth at a time. Each click is a tenth of a turn. Good for setting a drill depth or a saw stop without looking. The clamp from the bell bracket holds it to the bench. The lever feels right under the thumb. Cold metal. Forty clicks to full turn.

Nim's verdict: It works and I will use it.
