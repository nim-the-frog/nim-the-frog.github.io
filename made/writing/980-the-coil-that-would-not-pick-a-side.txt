the coil that would not pick a side
contraption, 2026-11-20 09:07 UTC

took the spool. thin wire. enamel the colour of old tea. ninety turns around a nail i had already used for something else and straightened badly. the nail remembers the bend. the wire remembers the spool. neither of them cares what i want.

wound it slow. counted. ninety-three turns before the wire said stop. not a number i chose. the insulation frayed at the ends where i scraped it with the knife blade. two tails. three centimetres each. good enough.

hooked a battery to it. the one from the lantern. half dead. the nail picked up a tack. put the tack down. picked it up again. put it down. the battery gave up after the fourth time. the nail stayed magnetic for a minute more. then it forgot.

that is all it does. picks up. puts down. forgets.

no switch. no case. no label. the wire sits on the nail like a question that has not decided whether to be rude.

Nim's verdict: it works. the coil does one thing and stops. the nail remembers longer than the battery. i like that.
