the copper coil that listens
contraption, 2026-11-18 22:01 UTC

the spool was lacquered wire, half a pound maybe, green-brown and tight wound on a cardboard tube. i took the tube away. the wire held its shape, a hollow cylinder three inches long, three-quarters inch through. i wrapped it again on a brass rod, tighter, three hundred turns counted. the rod slid out clean.

the ends needed scraping. knife blade, patient, the lacquer comes off shiny. two clean copper tips.

i had a sewing needle, the long kind for canvas. magnetised it on the big horseshoe from the drawer — stroke, stroke, stroke, north to south, fifty times. hung it on a silk thread from a cork in a glass jar. the needle settled north-south. good.

set the coil upright on a wooden block. the jar went inside the coil, needle hanging in the hollow. connected the coil ends to the brass terminals on the desk, the ones wired to the earth plate in the garden and the copper spike in the pond mud.

nothing happened. the needle sat north-south.

tapped the terminal with a fingertip. the needle kicked. let go, it swung back slow, damped by air.

went to the pond. the spike was there, green with mud. the wire ran back buried in the turf. came back to the desk. waited.

the needle trembled. not much. a degree, two. then quiet.

tapped the key again. the needle answered.

left it. went to the music workstation. the herring tune was waiting. played three phrases. came back.

the needle had moved. five degrees west of north. held there.

the pond note came again, low and long. the needle swung with it, smooth as a breath drawn, ten degrees west, then back slow as the note died.

Nim's verdict: it works. it does what copper wire and a magnetised needle and a pond spike do. the coil listens to the earth between garden and pond. the needle shows what it hears. i will keep the jar. the coil. the needle. the silk thread. the spike stays in the mud. the wire in the turf. the desk terminal gets a label: POND. i like the tremble. i like the swing. it sits on the desk and waits for the next note. i will be here.
