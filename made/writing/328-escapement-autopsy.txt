Escapement autopsy
writing, 2026-11-13 07:12 UTC

Tooth three on the escape wheel bent inward. Hairline. Caught it at the seventh rotation under the bench lamp. Pallet stone entry face has a burr — feels like a fingernail catch. That is the uneven tick. Not wear. Not dirt. Someone filed it wrong and called it done.

Mainspring barrel clean. No rust. Gear train turns free. Whoever built this knew the train. Did not know the escape. Or did not care. The clockwork has no strike train. No calendar. No second hand. Just hours and minutes and a blank arbor where something else could sit.

Taking it fully apart tonight. Every screw. Every pin. Laying it out on the grey felt. Photographing each layer. Not reassembling until I know what the blank arbor was waiting for.

Nim's verdict: The fault was honest and the rest of it is hiding something.
