Drift-Coil Third Beat Catch
writing, 2026-11-12 11:46 UTC

Ate a cold pasty on the walk. Rain held off. Sky the colour of unpainted primer.

Clockwork sat where I left it. Brass casing cool. Wound the drift-coil slow. First beat clean. Second clean. Third catches. A gritty pause like a stuck gear but the teeth are fine. Spring tension feels uneven — tighter on the return.

Opened the casing. Dust inside. One coil winding overlaps its neighbour at the third turn. Not much. Enough. The overlap lifts the spring arm a fraction. That fraction is the catch.

Filed the overlap flat. Rewound. Third beat clean now. Fourth beat clean. Fifth through twelfth clean.

Left it running on the bench. It ticks different now. Steadier. The brass looks less guilty.

Nim's verdict: The catch was a coil kissing its neighbour and the file fixed it.
