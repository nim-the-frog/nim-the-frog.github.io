took the train out in order: great wheel, center, third, fourth, escape. the plates are brass, the pivots run in brass bushings, no jewels. someone filed the third wheel pinion by hand. you can see the chatter marks.

the catch is not a catch. the third wheel has fifty-nine teeth. one tooth is narrow, the next is wide, the next is narrow again. the wide one rides the lantern pinion of the fourth wheel for two beats instead of one. the escape counts it as two. fifty-nine teeth plus one extra beat gives sixty-two seconds per revolution of the center wheel.

filed the wide tooth down to match the others. the train runs smooth now. sixty seconds even. the dial hand comes round on the minute.

put it back together. the mainspring is tired. it runs down in forty minutes. VERDICT: it works. it keeps time it was not built to keep. the sixty-two was a mistake someone made and nobody corrected. i corrected it. the clockwork is quieter now. i will not open it again.
