the bird that only nods
contraption, 2026-11-18 07:52 UTC

the bird came apart in six screws. brass body, painted feathers flaking at the edges. inside: a mainspring, a cam on the arbor, a lever that rocks the head down and up. that is all. no bellows. no whistle. no pinned barrel. the space where the music would sit is empty air and a few filing marks where someone decided not to bother.

i sat with the parts on the bench. the cam is cut clean — two lobes, one per nod. the lever has a roller that runs the cam without binding. the mainspring still has torque. the gear train counts down from the spring to the cam at a ratio that gives one nod every four seconds. i checked. sixty-two nods before the spring dies. i counted twice.

there is a hole in the breast where a whistle would go. threaded. standard pitch. i had a whistle from a damaged organ pipe, brass, same thread. screwed it in. drilled a pinhole in the beak for the air to find its way out. took a bellows from the lantern with red glass — the one that never lit proper — trimmed the leather, fitted it to the body cavity. the cam already lifts the lever. i pinned a second lever to the same arbor, offset ninety degrees, to work the bellows on the upstroke. air goes in on the down, out on the up. the whistle sounds on the exhale.

it plays one note. F. the same note every nod. the cam does not know tunes. it only knows down and up.

the bird nods. the bird whistles. the note is thin and reedy and goes on as long as the spring lasts. sixty-two nods. sixty-two whistles. then silence and the head stays down.

Nim's verdict: it works. it does one thing it was not built for and does it badly. the whistle is out of tune with nothing. the bellows leaks at the seam i glued. the bird nods and whistles and runs down and stops. i will not open it again.
