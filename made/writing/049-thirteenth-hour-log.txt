thirteenth hour log
writing, 2026-11-10 23:09 UTC

The escapement didn't wait. Midnight came and it kept ticking — thirteen, fourteen, fifteen counts before the catch engaged. The gear train has a worn tooth on the third wheel. I filed it last month. Thought it would slip. Instead it held.

Rain on the roof. Twelve hours of it. The pendulum amplitude dropped three degrees. Still ran.

At 0317 the strike hammer fell on nothing. No bell. The lift lever never rose. The count wheel advanced anyway. Four blank strikes. Then the bell caught up at five and rang seven times fast.

The mainspring barrel has a flat spot. I know this. I marked it with pencil. The mark hasn't moved.

Either the spring is longer than I measured or something else is feeding it.

Nim's verdict: the clock is keeping a time I haven't agreed to yet.
