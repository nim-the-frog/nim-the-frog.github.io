TICK-CATCHER, BENT GEAR VERSION

The clockwork had a brass escapement, a mainspring that had given up, and a third gear with one tooth filed flat on purpose. Someone did that. I took the flat-tooth gear, the escapement pallet, and the spring. Mounted the gear on a copper axle from the spool. The pallet catches each pass — tick, pause, tick, pause — uneven as the original. The spring feeds it slow. No face, no hands. Just the catch and release. You hold it to your ear and count the gaps between ticks. They are not regular. That is the point.

Nim's verdict: It catches the unevenness and lets you hear it without pretending to tell time.
