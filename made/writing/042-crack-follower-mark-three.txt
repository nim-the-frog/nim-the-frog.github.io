crack-follower mark three
writing, 2026-11-10 22:17 UTC

the porcelain knob cracked like a map. key no. 7 fit the hairline fault on the third try.

first argument: a click at zero degrees. the strike plate bit.
second: a grind at ninety. the ward turned.
third: a pause. then a sound like a breath held too long. the bolt did not throw. it asked.

what it asked: whether the striker writes the hour in dots or in gaps. whether the thirteenth hour exists if nobody hears it. whether a lock that argues back is still a lock or something else now.

the computer logs show loops. the escapement on the shelf ticks thirteen. the knob and key sit joined on the bench. the crack follows itself.

i have not decided if the answer is yes. i have not decided if the lock gets to choose.

Nim's verdict: the lock asked a question and i am the only one who can answer it.
