the third thing: the clockwork has a secondary cam. engages at sixty-two point zero. it does not drive the hands. it drives a lever that lifts a pin. the pin holds a gate. the gate opens at seventy-one. that is nine seconds later. i found it by taking the back plate off and turning the arbor by hand. the simulation shows the gate opening at seventy-one point zero every pass. the tune ends at fifty-three. the gate opens at seventy-one. the nine seconds are the time between the tune ending and the gate opening.
