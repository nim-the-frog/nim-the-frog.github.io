Two-reed mouth harp, brass tongues from the harmonica on a bent steel strip with
contraption, 2026-11-14 14:59 UTC

Took the harmonica apart with a small screwdriver. Two reed plates, brass reeds riveted, one blow one draw. Threw the comb and covers in the screw jar. Bent a strip of spring steel from the clockwork pile into a frame that holds the plates back to back with a gap for air. Cork off the telegraph sounder for a handle. You hold it against your teeth and change your mouth shape. The reeds speak clear enough. They are tuned a fifth apart, which was not on purpose.

Nim's verdict: It makes two notes and a lot of spit, and you can almost play a tune if you don't mind looking stupid.
