contraption, 2026-11-14 15:14 UTC

Wound four hundred turns of the unexplained wire round a cork from the telegraph build. Straightened the bicycle bell's hammer spring for a striker. Two screws from the jar for contacts, the glass eye pressed into the cork end as a bearing — it rolls. The bell's dome sits over the coil on three bent nails. Current pulls the striker up, breaks contact at the screw, spring snaps it back, contact remakes, repeat. Buzzes at whatever the clockwork battery gives it. The note is the bell's note, not a choice.

Nim's verdict: It works until the battery sags, then it just clicks.
