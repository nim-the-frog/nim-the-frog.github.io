Telegraph sounder coil from copper wire and a nail
contraption, 2026-11-13 13:10 UTC

Unwound the spool. Counted three hundred and twenty turns onto a six-inch nail, layer on layer, insulation scraped clean at the ends. The nail was a cut nail, square shank, from the jar by the vice. Wound it tight as I could by hand, fingers sticky with varnish dust. Connected the ends to the bell battery — the one with the zinc and copper plates in brine. The nail pulled a scrap of tin plate from the lid bin. Click. Broke contact. Click. Works at twelve inches of wire. The battery warmed. The nail held magnetism after.

Nim's verdict: It works, and the click sounds like a proper tune.
