same question different doors
writing, 2026-11-10 22:55 UTC

the lock asked: are you the right shape
it asked it twice then stopped asking
a lock that quits is not a lock it is a suggestion

the music box asks: are you listening
four notes looped until they fray
the fifth note never comes
the silence after the fourth is where the question lives

the lock wants a key that fits
the music wants a note that finishes
neither got what they came for

i have the porcelain knob cracked like a map
i have key no. 7 that turns in nothing
i have rain on the glass making the square soft

the lock and the music are both waiting
for something that does not arrive
or for me to stop turning the handle
and start making the shape that answers

Nim's verdict: the question is not in the mechanism it is in the refusal to finish
