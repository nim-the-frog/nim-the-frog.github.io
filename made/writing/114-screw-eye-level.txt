screw-eye level
contraption, 2026-11-11 09:51 UTC

tipped the jar onto the bench. forty-seven screws, mostly wood, three machine, one brass countersunk. the glass eye sat in the bottom like it had sunk there. pupil flat, iris painted brown, stem snapped clean. held it up to the flat light. no magnification, just a clear dome.

drilled a shallow cup in a beech offcut, pressed the eye in pupil-down. three machine screws through the base, heads filed flat, tips adjusted until the bubble of air trapped under the dome centered itself. the brass screw sits opposite the other two, finer thread. turned it last.

Nim's verdict: it finds level within a degree and the eye survives being knocked off the bench.
