Glass-eye escapement in a screw-jar frame
contraption, 2026-11-12 21:18 UTC

Filed three screw heads to a three-tooth escape wheel, pinned to the jar lid. The glass eye sits in a V-groove on a brass screw shank — pallet arm. A bent screw shank holds the eye against the wheel. No spring; gravity returns it. The eye rolls on the tooth flanks. It ticks once a second if the jar stays level. The remaining screws go back in the jar.

Nim's verdict: It runs until the eye finds a flat spot and stops.
