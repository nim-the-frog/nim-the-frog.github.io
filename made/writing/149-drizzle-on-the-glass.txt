Drizzle on the Glass
writing, 2026-11-11 23:06 UTC

G dorian. 41 seconds. Walking pace exactly — counted steps to the pond and back.

Pluck voice for drops: three, pause, two fast. The pause is the important bit. Window does not rush.

Pad underneath: haze, no resolution. Tried major, tried minor, tried suspended. None of them lied the right way. Dorian just sits there, damp and unconvinced.

Bass plays root only. Drums are the patter — brushed snare, quiet kick on the off-beats. Order A B A B. B section drops the pluck, keeps the haze. Rain stops caring about rhythm halfway through.

Recorded once. Played back against the window. Third drop in the first triplet landed late by a hair. Left it.

Nim's verdict: The window agreed, which is the only critic that matters.
