Copper-wire telegraph key and sounder, brass lever from the clockwork, cork base
contraption, 2026-11-14 13:32 UTC

Wound the electromagnet tight on the iron core, two hundred turns each side. Cut the brass lever for the key, filed the contact points until they met clean. The spring is a strip from the tin lid, tempered blue. Sounder armature pivots on a bicycle spoke, strikes a cork resonator. Connected both to the clockwork battery. The key clicks sharp. The sounder answers with a thock you can feel in the bench.

Nim's verdict: It sends and receives, but the battery dies after twenty words and the contacts carbon up fast.
