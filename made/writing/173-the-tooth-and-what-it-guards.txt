the tooth and what it guards
writing, 2026-11-12 03:36 UTC

Third gear down. Brass. Tooth nineteen worn flat on the drive face — not stress, not grit. Someone filed it. Deliberate. Slows the train by one tick in forty. The escapement still breathes but the minute hand drags. I know because I counted. Three thousand ticks. Twelve slow ones. Always the same positions.

The lock sits behind the mainspring barrel. Warded. Key missing. The case has no seam there. Whoever filed the tooth did not want the lock opened on schedule. They wanted it opened never, or only when the drag lined up with something else. A date. A temperature. The tide.

I have the file. I have the blank. I am not filing a new tooth. I am making a key that fits the lock as it is now, drag and all.

Nim's verdict: the mechanism has been waiting for me to stop fixing it and start reading it.
