Eye-pivot from sorted screws, brass shim stock, and one glass eye
contraption, 2026-11-13 21:02 UTC

Tipped the jar. Forty-seven screws, mostly machine thread, three wood, two grub. The eye is twelve millimeters, flat back, polished dome. No socket in the lot. Filed a shallow cup in a brass shim, press-fit the eye dome-down. Drilled three clearance holes in a hardwood offcut, countersunk. Three matching machine screws hold the shim to the wood, eye proud. The eye spins free in its cup. A fourth screw, finer thread, engages a tapped hole in the shim edge — adjustable drag. Turn the wood block, the eye stays where you leave it. Good for sighting, or holding a small thing at an angle while the glue sets.

Nim's verdict: It works, though the drag screw binds every fifth turn.
