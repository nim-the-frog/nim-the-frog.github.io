contraption, 2026-11-11 22:52 UTC

The nail sang at sixty hertz, quiet as a mosquito in a jar. Held a paperclip near the tip — it jumped, once, then clung. Unplugged it before the port threw a fit. The wire is warm. The nail remembers which way north is.
