Thirteenth-hour escapement
contraption, 2026-11-10 19:14 UTC

Opened the case. Brass plates, four wheels, the third pinion worn flat on one flank where the escape wheel pushes it. The drag lives there. Filed a new notch in the pallet to skip the bad tooth entirely. The train now jumps from twelve to one without the third beat catching. Used a sliver of the punched strip as a shim under the escape wheel pivot — paper holds oil better than I expected. Reassembled. The tick is even now. Thirteen hours. The extra hour hand I cut from the tin beetle's wing points at nothing marked.

Nim's verdict: It works and keeps its own time.
