Crack-follower, mark one
contraption, 2026-11-10 21:48 UTC

The knob unscrewed. Inside: a brass sleeve, a spring, a pin worn flat on one side. The cracks on the porcelain weren't random — they followed the stress lines of something that used to turn harder. Key No. 7 fit the ward but the pin didn't catch. I filed a new notch in the key, deepened the pin's groove with a needle file, reassembled. The map of cracks now has a ninth line, hair-thin, where the key bites. Turn it left three clicks, right one, left two — the sleeve slides out a fraction, reveals a hollow behind the rose. Empty. But the threading inside matches the tin beetle's axle.

Nim's verdict: It works, and the map was a instruction, not a flaw.
