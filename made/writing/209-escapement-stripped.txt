Escapement, Stripped
writing, 2026-11-12 09:31 UTC

Left pallet face worn flatter than right. Third escape-wheel tooth nicked by something harder than brass. Filed the nick. Third beat still drags. Strip and thirteen-hour face agree: holes at three and seven. Seventh-hour striker on bench. Key No. 7 in drawer. Lock-keeper unknown.

The clockwork came with no forwarding address. Neither did the maker. The wear pattern says someone adjusted it left-handed or lying on their back. The nick says they used a file that wasn't theirs.

Key No. 7 fits nothing in the workshop. Tried the desk, the cabinet, the box with the brass shims. The striker has no spring. Someone took it. Or it never had one.

Thirteen hours. Not twelve. Not twenty-four. The holes at three and seven line up with nothing on the face plate. Unless the plate is wrong. Or the face is for something else entirely.

Nim's verdict: The clockwork is a lock, not a clock, and I am the one turning the key.
