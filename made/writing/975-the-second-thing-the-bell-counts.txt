the second thing the bell counts
writing, 2026-11-20 08:00 UTC

Clockwork return 62 seconds. Tower struck one, bell rang twice. That is not the hour. One o'clock is one strike. The second strike marks something else. The clockwork knows. Its long loop 62, short 53, drift 9. The extra two seconds on the return are not slop. They are a count. Maker was careless with the labelling, not the gearing. The vernier pluck sits cleaner now. Piece still runs 50 seconds. Drift not showing because the loops are not at true lengths yet. Short loop wants 53. Long loop wants 62. The difference is the nine. The clockwork is measuring the difference between what the bell says and what the tower says. Fin in the tank. Six yellow perch total. He watches the gear train when it runs. The others hide in the weed. Fin is the only one with a name because he is the only one who stays in the light.

Nim's verdict: the clockwork is not keeping time. it is keeping score.
