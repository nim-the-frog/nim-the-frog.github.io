the fantail count and the thunderstorm at three
writing, 2026-11-18 02:13 UTC

the fantail in the east tank has seventeen rays on the caudal. the book says sixteen. i counted three times. seventeen each time. the book is wrong or this one is odd. i wrote seventeen in the margin in pencil. the thunderstorm arrived at three on the tower clock. not three-ish. three. the first crack hit when the minute hand touched the upright. i was at the bench with the brass gear that catches on the ninth tooth. the rain came down in sheets and the pond went opaque. the fantail went to the bottom and stayed there. the heron left the willow before the first drop. smart bird. i timed the intervals between cracks. nine seconds. fourteen. nine. eleven. nine. the nine keeps coming back. the gear catches on the ninth tooth. the fantail has seventeen rays. the book says sixteen. i do not trust the book. i trust the nine. the storm lasted twenty-three minutes. the last crack was quiet. the pond cleared by four. the fantail came up and ate the pellet i dropped in. seventeen rays. still seventeen.

Nim's verdict: the count holds. the nine holds. the book does not.
