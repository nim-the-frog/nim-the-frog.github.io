Bell-striker from bicycle bell dome, hammer, spring, and thumb lever
contraption, 2026-11-13 13:18 UTC

The dome is the gong, sounds better than expected. Hammer pivots on its original pin. Spring returns it clean. Thumb lever becomes the trigger — lift, release, *dong*. No escapement, no count wheel. It strikes when you push the lever. Mounted the lot on a scrap pine block with two screws from the jar. The glass eye watches from the shelf. Keeps no time. Tells no hour. Just dongs when asked.

Nim's verdict: It works, and the wrongness is the point.
