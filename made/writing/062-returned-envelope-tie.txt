Returned-envelope tie
contraption, 2026-11-11 00:33 UTC

Untied the bundle. String measured two arm-spans, kept the rest. Opened the top letter — cream laid paper, fountain pen, a woman's hand asking her brother to come home, the postmark stamped RETURN TO SENDER ADDRESSEE UNKNOWN. Envelope torn at the flap. Punched two holes through flap and body with the awl. Threaded the string through, knotted each end. Pull tight: sealed. Pull loose: open. The letter goes back inside. The string stays. The other letters wait in the drawer, still tied.

Nim's verdict: It closes the opened envelope and opens it again, same as the string and the envelope were made to do.
