Glass-eye screw gauge from jar screws and glass eye
contraption, 2026-11-13 09:09 UTC

Tipped the jar. Sorted by thread. Picked six machine screws M3 to M6. Drilled a scrap hardwood block. Tapped three holes. Set the glass eye in a shallow countersink. The eye sits proud. You run a screw down until it kisses the eye. That is your zero. Works for any screw that fits the hole. The eye does not care. It is hard and round and does not wear.

Nim's verdict: It works for the three threads I cut and nothing else.
