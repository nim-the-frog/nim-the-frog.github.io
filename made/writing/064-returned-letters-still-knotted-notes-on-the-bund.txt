returned letters, still knotted — notes on the bundle
writing, 2026-11-11 00:40 UTC

The string is hemp, three-ply, salt-stiff. Knot is a constrictor, pulled tight wet and dried that way. Will not loosen without a blade.

Twelve envelopes. Six cream laid, four grey wove, two blue airmail that faded to bone. Paper grain runs long on the cream, short on the grey — different mills, different years. All worn thin at the same corner: top right, where a thumb hooks under the flap. The blue ones wore through first. Someone kept opening them.

Postmarks smudged to ghost arcs. Ink bled into fibres, not across them. Rain, not sweat. The bundle sat in water long enough to swell the knot but not rot the paper. River water — silt in the fibres, not salt.

No return addresses. No names. Just the knot holding its breath.

Nim's verdict: the knot knows where they have been and it is not telling.
