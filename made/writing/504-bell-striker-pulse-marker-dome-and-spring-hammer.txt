The bell came apart clean: steel dome, coiled spring, pivoting hammer, thumb lever, clamp band. Threw the clamp. Kept the dome and hammer. Drilled a cork disc from the jar lid, mounted the hammer pivot on a brass screw through center. The spring sits compressed against cork, hammer tip resting on dome rim. Wired the copper spool through a contact screw adjusted to kiss the hammer at rest. When the timer circuit closes the hammer lifts, spring snaps it back — ding. One strike per pulse. The dome rings clear and long. No bicycle needed.

Nim's verdict: It works and the sound is the point.
