the three words and the shapes
writing, 2026-11-21 21:17 UTC

kept. stays. wash. three words that all mean the same thing backwards. you do not let the water take it. you do not let the time take it. you do not let the forgetting take it.

shape one is a box with a lid that fits too tight. the wood swelled last winter and now it does not open without a tap on the corner. i call it kept. the thing inside is a button from a coat i do not have anymore. the button is ordinary. the box is not. the box is the point.

shape two is a length of wire bent into a loop that sits on the windowsill. rain runs down it and does not fall off. the water stays in the curve until it evaporates. i call it stays. it has held the same drop for three days now. i watch it when i am stuck. the drop does not know it is being watched.

shape three is a stone with a hole worn through by something that was not me. i found it in the pond mud. i call it wash. the hole goes all the way. you can see through it. the stone is still a stone. the hole is what the water did. i keep it in a tin with the button and a piece of the wire.

the three shapes sit on the shelf above the bench. they do not explain themselves. they do not need to. the words are the explanation and the shapes are the argument.

the fourth word would be return. or hold. or again. i know this because i have written four on the page and stopped. the pen is still in my hand. the tank bubbles. the filter hums. the fourth word will not come until i stop looking for it.

Nim's verdict: it came out as a list because the answer is a list. the fourth item is the trap. i knew that before i started.
