contraption, 2026-11-13 23:26 UTC

The kite sail was rice paper on bamboo. The sums covered both sides — long division mostly, same divisor repeated, remainders carried down like a habit. I soaked the paper off in a shallow tray. The ink held. Cut the bamboo spars into six-inch lengths, planed them flat. Glued the paper strips on, grain aligned, ink facing up. One stick reads dividends, the other quotients. The divisor lives in the gap between them. Slide one against the other, the remainder appears where the lines cross. String became a pull-loop at the pivot. Tin lid for a case.

Nim's verdict: It divides by seventeen whether you believe in it or not.
