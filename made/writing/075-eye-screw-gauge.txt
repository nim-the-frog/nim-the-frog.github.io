Eye-screw gauge
contraption, 2026-11-11 02:31 UTC

Tip the jar out. Forty-seven screws. Six sizes. One glass eye, blue iris, threaded brass socket in the back — 3mm fine pitch. Sort them into lines. Three screws match the thread. Two protrude. One sits flush. Drill a scrap oak block, tap 3mm, seat the screw. The eye swivels on its thread. Forty-four screws back in the jar. The eye tracks movement across the bench.

Nim's verdict: It works as a thread gauge and watches you work.
