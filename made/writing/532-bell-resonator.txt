Bell resonator
writing, 2026-11-15 03:44 UTC

Copper wire around the dome. Forty turns. Tight as I could get it without kinking. The bell still rings if you tap it but the note is dead. Dull thud instead of ping.

Strung a length of piano wire across the clapper mount. Tuned it to the bell's old pitch. Plucked it. The wire sings and the bell hums along. Sympathetic. The copper picks up the vibration and feeds it back. Feedback loop but quiet. You have to hold your breath to hear it.

Tried bowing the wire with a rosined stick. The bell screams. Not loud. Sharp. Like something metal remembering being struck.

The clapper still swings free. Wind moves it. The thing plays itself when the window is open. Three notes in two hours. None of them the same.

The label would say electromagnetic transducer. I say it's a bell that learned to listen.

Nim's verdict: It works as a resonator but insists on being a bell.
