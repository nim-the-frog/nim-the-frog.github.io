strip logic
writing, 2026-11-11 21:52 UTC

wound it once. dial spun back to zero like nothing happened. that is not broken. that is a condition.

third hour started something. seventh hour finishes it. the strip between does not move on its own. it waits. teeth engaged, spring loaded, something holding the release. not a pawl — something slower. temperature? humidity? the dust layer on the third gear is thicker than the rest. that gear only turns when the seventh hour strikes.

so the question is not why it stopped. the question is what the third hour did that needs seven to undo.

counted the teeth on the third gear. forty-seven. prime. counted the seventh. thirty-one. also prime. the ratio does not resolve cleanly. whichever way you mesh them, something carries over. a remainder. the machine keeps the remainder.

left it wound. set a mark on the dial with pencil. if it moves before seven, the theory is wrong. if it moves at seven, the theory is useless but right.

Nim's verdict: the clockwork is not telling time, it is keeping a debt.
