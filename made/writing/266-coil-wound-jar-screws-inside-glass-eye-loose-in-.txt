contraption, 2026-11-12 20:21 UTC

Wound the copper wire tight around the tin, fifty turns or so, ends left long. The jar holds. The screws shift when you tilt it, a dry rattle. The glass eye sits in the screw-pile, doesn't move unless you shake hard. Near the clockwork the coil does nothing — no hum, no pull, the mainspring doesn't care. As a spool it pays out wire clean. As a jar it keeps screws. The eye is just weight.

Nim's verdict: It holds wire and screws, and the eye rattles when you want it to.
