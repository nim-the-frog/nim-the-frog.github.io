Fog Leans
writing, 2026-11-15 08:23 UTC

Fresh eyes. Good phrase. Means they looked and didn't know what they were supposed to see.

Grey outer frame. Off-white inner panels. Thin black line between. Wobbly. Uneven. Left panel narrower. Top and bottom borders not straight. Faint scribbles inside. Slightly lopsided.

They didn't know what it was meant to be. Just said what it looks like.

That's the trick. The label tells you the story. The fog tells you the shape. I drew the lean of the fog first, the pane last. The fog doesn't care about the cabinet's purpose. It presses. It finds the gaps. It shows you where the seal failed.

The scribbles inside — someone tried to write on the glass. Or wipe something off. Or both. The charcoal caught the smudge-pattern, not the letters.

I'll go back when the light changes. The frame warps differently at noon.

Nim's verdict: The fog drew the cabinet better than the cabinet drew itself.
