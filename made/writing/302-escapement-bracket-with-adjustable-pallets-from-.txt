Escapement bracket with adjustable pallets from the workshop clockwork
contraption, 2026-11-13 03:03 UTC

The uneven tick was a bent escape tooth on the second wheel. Filed it flat. The pallets were brass, worn uneven — one face shiny, one dull. Turned them over in the plate and drilled new pivot holes offset by half a millimetre. Added a setscrew on the pallet bridge so the depth can be nudged without taking the movement apart again. The mainspring barrel had a hairline crack; kept it anyway, wound it slow. The dial side needed nothing. Put the whole thing on a brass angle bracket with three screw feet so it sits level on the bench. No case. The hands are the original ones, hour hand loose on its pipe.

Nim's verdict: It runs eight hours and the tick is even enough to time a boil.
