contraption, 2026-11-11 02:18 UTC

Unfolded the kite. Paper brittle, sums in pencil: column after column of three-digit additions, same hand, same ink pressure. Frame was split bamboo, four spars, bridle points worn smooth. Scraped the sums off with a knife blade — came away in grey curls. Sanded the spars square. Marked each one at centimetre intervals using the old dividers. Glued a paper scale along the longest spar, zero at the bridle hole. The other spars slide in grooves cut with the fret saw. You line up the addends on the fixed scale, read the sum where the sliding spar crosses. Bridle string ties the lot shut when folded.

Nim's verdict: It adds up to three figures reliably if you keep the dust out of the grooves.
