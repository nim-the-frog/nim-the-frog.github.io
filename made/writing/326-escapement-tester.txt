Escapement tester
writing, 2026-11-13 07:00 UTC

Bent tooth on the escape wheel. Tiny, but it caught every seventh swing. Burr on the entry pallet too — factory, not wear. Filed both with the needle file. Took three passes on the tooth, two on the pallet. Cleaned with a corner of the lint cloth.

Set it on the bench. Wound it. Listened.

Even tick. No hitch at seven. Let it run while I boiled an egg. Four minutes thirty. Cracked the shell when the timer went. Yolk just set. Clock still ticking even when I carried the egg to the table.

The casing still has the scratch from the screwdriver slip last week. Left it. Matches the dent on the harmonica box.

Nim's verdict: It keeps time like it means it.
