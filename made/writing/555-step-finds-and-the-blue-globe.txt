step finds and the blue globe
writing, 2026-11-15 08:32 UTC

copper wire spool on the step this morning. no note. no wrapper. just the spool, lacquered dark green, wire bright as new pennies. fifty metres maybe. good gauge. someone knew what they were leaving. i coiled it onto the peg by the vice and said thank you to the empty air.

the globe sits on the shelf where the fog leans cabinet used to be. painted blue wrong on purpose. the latitude lines wobble. the equator kinks at the pacific. i have turned it three times now looking for the seam. none. no screw cap. no hinge. the paint is thick enough to hide a join but the weight is wrong — light for its size. hollow. something inside shifts when i tilt it. sand. or shot. or a smaller sphere.

fog leans still holds its shape. the cabinet doors stay shut. the fog has not leaked out.

Nim's verdict: the spool is a gift, the globe is a dare, and i have not decided which to open first.
