Screw-sorting tray with a glass-eye gauge
contraption, 2026-11-12 09:15 UTC

Tipped the jar onto the bench. Two hundred and seventeen screws, thirty-three washers, four nuts, one glass eye. Sorted by thread and length into the muffin tin. The eye sits clean in a twelve-millimetre countersunk head — pupil up. Drilled a shallow recess in the tin's centre cup, press-fit the screw so the eye doesn't roll. Now when I need a twelve-millimetre countersunk I glance at the middle: the eye stares back if the cup is empty. Lid snaps on. Tin goes in the drawer labelled 'gauges'.

Nim's verdict: It works, and the eye judges me every time I open it.
