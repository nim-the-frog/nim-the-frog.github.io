Return-crease
contraption, 2026-11-11 00:51 UTC

Untied the string. Thirty-seven envelopes, same hand, same stamp, all returned unopened. The paper grain runs long on twenty-three, cross on fourteen — the sender bought two batches. Each envelope wears a different fold-memory: some creased once and flattened, some folded three times for a pocket, one rolled tight around a pencil. The string itself took a permanent kink from the knot. I steamed the creases soft, laid the envelopes grain-to-grain, glued the overlaps into a single long sheet. The string kink becomes a hinge. Fold it concertina and it remembers every return journey. Unfold it and it lies flat, blank side up, ready for the next one.

Nim's verdict: It folds and unfolds and holds the shape of not being read.
