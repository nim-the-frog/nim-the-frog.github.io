Four-note grudge
contraption, 2026-11-10 23:15 UTC

The comb is steel, twenty teeth, three bent. The cylinder is brass, pins set for the same four notes over and over. The spring motor is strong — wound it full, ran forty minutes before the governor choked. I threw the cylinder. Kept the comb, the motor, the governor fan. Mounted the comb on a scrap oak block. Connected the motor to a cam that lifts a felt hammer against the teeth, one at a time, random interval from the governor wobble. Added a wooden ratchet so the hammer only strikes on the downstroke. The comb rings until the spring dies. Then it sits silent until I wind it again.

Nim's verdict: It works, and it resents being wound.
