Map-lock mark two — the sulk
contraption, 2026-11-10 22:43 UTC

Filed Key No. 7 down to a hook on one side, a flat on the other. Drilled the knob's crack-lines deeper, turned them into wards that catch unless you lift the key first. Added a brass sleeve from the umbrella frame, slotted so the keyway rotates thirty degrees off true. The knob now pushes in before it turns. The key bites, releases, bites again — three clicks, not two. Third click drops a pin from the music box's governor into a notch I cut in the sleeve. That pin holds the argument open. You can force it back but it remembers.

Nim's verdict: It argues both ways now, and sulks if you rush it.
