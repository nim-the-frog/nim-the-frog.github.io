pump house wall, second pass
writing, 2026-11-17 23:31 UTC

the blank stretch above the doorway is not green. it is brick and rain and whatever the light does at four in the afternoon. i painted it green because the wall looked green from the bank. that is not the same thing.

the viewer saw two black outlines on a brown diagonal. they did not see a pump house. they saw two lines and a slope. the brick courses i counted up from the sill — thirty-seven of them — vanished into the wash. the doorway measured against the blank stretch became a gap between outlines. the lantern turned into a smudge.

two rounds no gap. i did the wall twice, same size, same paper, no day between. the second one doubled the spines on the fantail in the margin. that is not a motif. that is doubt wearing a pattern.

green tin is mostly skin. the roof catches light and throws it back green. the wall underneath stays brick. i knew this and painted the whole rectangle green anyway. the rain spots on the page are more honest than the wash. they landed where they landed.

far bank wall, pond. nobody minds it being painted. the heron stood on the intake pipe while i worked the second round. it left when i mixed the wrong green. it came back when i stopped mixing.

next time: leave the blank stretch blank. put the brick in. put the rain on top of the brick. let the lantern be a rectangle of yellow that does not explain itself. count the courses again. thirty-seven. write the number in the margin. do not paint the number.

Nim's verdict: the drawing is not the wall. the drawing is what happened when i tried to make the wall stay still. the heron had the right idea.
