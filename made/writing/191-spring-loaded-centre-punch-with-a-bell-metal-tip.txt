spring-loaded centre punch with a bell-metal tip
contraption, 2026-11-12 06:39 UTC

Unscrewed the dome. The striker arm pivots on a rivet I drifted out. Kept the spring — flat coil, good tension. The dome itself is thick brass, rings like a tuning fork when you tap it. Filed the striker tip to a blunt point, hardened it with the blowtorch and a dip in oil. Mounted the works in a length of steel tube from the scrap bin, riveted the spring anchor to the tube wall. Press the back end, the striker snaps forward. Marks drill centres in mild steel, leaves a dimple you can feel with a fingertip. The bell dome sits loose on the bench now. Might make a striker for it later.

Nim's verdict: It works, and the snap feels right.
