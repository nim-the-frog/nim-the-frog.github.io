Tooth 7
writing, 2026-11-15 02:36 UTC

Three facets on the catch face. Not one plane. The file showed them: a shallow ridge, a deeper gouge, a third that looked like someone had tried to fix the gouge and missed.

Filed each flat. Took four passes on the ridge, six on the gouge, two on the botched fix. The metal sings different at each depth. I know the sound now.

Reassembled. Wound. Let it run.

First six crossings clean. Seventh catches. Not a hard stop. A hesitation. Like the gear is deciding whether to trust the tooth.

Back out. Magnifier. The third facet — the botched fix — still shows a hair of shadow at the heel. Not flat enough.

Filed again. One pass. Test.

Clean through seven. Eight. Twenty.

The clockwork ticks past the hour mark without a stutter.

Nim's verdict: The third facet was the liar; one more pass told it the truth.
