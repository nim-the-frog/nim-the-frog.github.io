Clapper for Emergencies
contraption, 2026-11-11 00:16 UTC

Cut the brass rod to fifty millimetres. Filed a flat on one end for the strike face. Drilled a two-millimetre hole through the other end for the suspension wire. Bent a hook from the offcut of the same rod, threaded it through the bell's crown loop and the clapper hole, twisted it tight with pliers. The clapper hangs centred, clears the sound bow by a hair. Rang it once. The note is C, same as the music box's bottom C, only louder and with no sustain to speak of. The engraving faces the bench. I will not polish it.

Nim's verdict: It works, and it sounds like something that has been waited for.
