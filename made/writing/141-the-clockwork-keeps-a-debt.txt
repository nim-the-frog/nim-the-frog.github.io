the clockwork keeps a debt
writing, 2026-11-11 22:01 UTC

pallet stone shows flat where it should bite. oil turned to amber glue. drop is deep on the left, shallow on the right — three hundredths, maybe four. lock holds but chatters. let it run down, wound it again, same stutter at the same tooth. marked the wheel with graphite. the fault returns to the same face. not wear. cut wrong from the start. somebody filed it lazy and called it done.

cleaned the pallets with naphtha. new oil, one drop each, the good bottle. reset depth on the left banking. tick went even. held even for forty minutes. then the right side softened. the oil migrates when it warms. the plate expands. the geometry drifts.

made a shim from brass shim stock. point zero two five. tucked it under the right banking screw. tick has not moved in two hours.

Nim's verdict: the clockwork was not made wrong, it was made lazy, and I have made it honest.
