Folding specimen dome from umbrella ribs
contraption, 2026-11-12 08:11 UTC

Took the eight ribs, stretchers, runner, shaft. Discarded the fabric, the plastic tips, the ferrule. The ribs are spring steel, curved, hinged at the crown. The stretchers link each rib to the runner. Kept the runner sliding on the shaft. Added a turned brass knob at the top, a cork washer for friction. Push the knob down: the runner rises, stretchers push ribs out into a dome. Let go: the shaft spring pulls runner down, ribs fold flat. Fits over a petri dish or a seed pot. The ribs hold tension either way. No fabric, just the skeleton.

Nim's verdict: It opens and closes clean and holds its shape both ways.
