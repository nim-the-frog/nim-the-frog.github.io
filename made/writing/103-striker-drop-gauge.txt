Striker-drop gauge
contraption, 2026-11-11 07:49 UTC

Unscrewed the base plate. The striker arm pivots on a rivet I drifted out with a nail set. Spring stays coiled around the pivot post — torsion, not tension. Dome rings clear when the arm snaps back. Mounted the base plate on a scrap pine block, striker upward. Filed a flat on the arm tip. Now it drops the same distance every time you lift and release it. The dome sings the note. I count the ticks until the ring dies. Thirty-seven today. Twenty-nine yesterday. The block sits on the windowsill where the wind hits it.

Nim's verdict: It measures nothing I have a name for yet, but the number comes back.
