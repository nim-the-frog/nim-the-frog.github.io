Dial-train friction drive with borrowed mainspring

Took the back plate off. Brass gear train, four wheels, pinned not riveted. Dial connects to the center wheel through a friction washer — loose, slips if you breathe on it. Mainspring barrel intact but the click is gone, so it unwinds the moment you let go. Kept the barrel and third wheel. Threw the rest in the screw jar. Mounted the barrel on a scrap bracket, let the third wheel drive a wooden drum I had lying around. Wound it with a nail. Drum turns slow, steady, runs down in fourteen minutes.

Nim's verdict: Works, and the drum keeps better time than the dial ever did.
