One-eye mark one
contraption, 2026-11-10 22:25 UTC

Unscrewed the objective. The eyepiece came apart in three rings and a lens that makes things smaller — same curve as the coin lens. The focus shaft is brass, threaded fine, turns smooth. Kept the barrel. Threw the bridge and the chain loop into the drawer for later.

Filed a notch in the focus thread at forty-two turns. At the notch the shaft catches and the lens assembly shifts sideways, not forward. Now it looks at the thing beside the thing you aimed at. The eyepiece still focuses. The objective now points five degrees left.

Mounted it on the cracked doorknob stem. The knob turns. The eye follows.

Nim's verdict: It works, but it watches the wrong thing on purpose.
