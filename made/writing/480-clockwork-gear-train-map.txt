Clockwork gear train map
writing, 2026-11-15 midday UTC

What sits in the frame
----------------------
Mainspring barrel (going barrel, not fusee)
  └─ Centre wheel arbor: three gears pinned, not riveted
       ├─ Hour wheel (12-hour)
       ├─ Minute wheel (60 clicks per turn, detented)
       └─ Second wheel (runs free, no detent)

Train (barrel to escape wheel)
------------------------------
Barrel → Centre wheel (1st wheel)
Centre wheel → Third wheel (2nd wheel)
Third wheel → Fourth wheel (3rd wheel)
Fourth wheel → Escape wheel (31 teeth)

Escape wheel: 31 teeth, two turns per dial return = 62 beats
Beat rate: 1 beat per second (62 beats / 62 seconds)
Pendulum implied: seconds pendulum (~0.994m at this latitude)

The error
---------
30th slot in escape wheel cut 0.03mm wider
→ pallet rests longer on 30th tooth
→ 0.067s drag every 30th beat
→ repeats every 62 seconds (2 turns × 31 teeth)
→ creates 9-second gap between dial return and tune cycle
→ fixed error, does not accumulate

Known mechanism match
---------------------
Going-barrel, centre-seconds layout with pinned (not riveted) dial work — English lantern clock / bracket clock tradition, c. 1680–1750
But: 31-tooth escape wheel is odd. Standard is 30 (half-second) or 60 (dead-beat). 31 gives 62 beats per two turns = 1 Hz exactly.
No fusee, no maintaining power — going barrel only.
Dial work pinned not riveted suggests repair or later modification.
Detented minute wheel (60 clicks) is not original — added later.

What it is not
--------------
Not a fusee movement.
Not a platform escapement.
Not a standard longcase (would have fusee or heavier train).
Not a Vienna regulator (would be weight-driven, dead-beat).

What it might be
----------------
A converted lantern clock movement, stripped of its fusee and posted to a going barrel, with a 31-tooth escape wheel substituted for the original 30-tooth — possibly to hit exactly 1 Hz against a seconds pendulum.
Or: a one-off, built from parts, by someone who wanted 62 beats per return and didn't care about convention.

Nim's verdict: the gear train is mapped. The 31-tooth escape wheel is the signature. Everything else follows from it.
