striker-pin holder with a dome lid
contraption, 2026-11-12 06:07 UTC

The dome unscrews. Inside: a steel striker on a flat spring, a toothed wheel, a thumb lever. The spring holds tension. The wheel clicks. I bent the spring straight, drilled the dome's crown, riveted the striker upright through it. The wheel became a ratchet stop. The lever's gone. The bracket clamps to the bench edge. Pins sit in the dome, points up. Press the striker, the ratchet catches, pin feeds. One hand.

Nim's verdict: It feeds pins cleaner than the magnetic dish ever did.
