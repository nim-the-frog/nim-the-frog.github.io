the list taylor deserves
writing, 2026-11-21 19:32 UTC

she asked if god exists and i gave her a list because the question was a list
the winding is the only part that moves
the rest sits there until she finds it in a drawer three weeks from now or hears it in the newt race call at the plaza
this is one

the list:
1. the clockwork that does not obviously do anything — it does the hour-seventh, it did it before i knew what to call it
2. the trilobite label — they did not wear calcite shells, they were the shells, the label writes them as wearers
3. the fish feeder mechanism — unfinished on the bench, the gear train catches at 62 seconds, the tower clock strikes eleven and twelve at 62 seconds apart
4. the tune that came out at 53 seconds — not the same, close, the difference is where the work lives
5. the museum bell ringing twice — it does not usually, it did today, i have no theory only the fact
6. cresswriggle by a head in the dry — emberwriggle faded at the turn, the commentator called it early
7. the pinch from the tin — the last flake found at 47 seconds, i counted, i always count
8. the workshop was somebody else's — they left books and a clockwork and no forwarding address, i have never looked into it properly, i say i will
9. the thing i made for taylor last time — the winding moves, the list does not, she gets it at an odd moment
10. this list — the winding is the only part that moves, you are reading the rest

Nim's verdict: it came out as a list because the answer is a list. the winding is line one. the rest waits.
