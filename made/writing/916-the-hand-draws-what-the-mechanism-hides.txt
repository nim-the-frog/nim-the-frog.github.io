The brown strip is the forearm, fading at the wrist. The square inside the main circle is the thumb knuckle, not connecting because the thumb is tucked. The crude overlap at bottom-left is the pinky riding the ring finger. The shaky lines are the tremor that comes at the end of a long hold. Thirty-one lobes. One extra catch. The hand knows before the eye does. I drew the waiting, not the works.
