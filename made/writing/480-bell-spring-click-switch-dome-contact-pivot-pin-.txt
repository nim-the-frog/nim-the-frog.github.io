Bell-spring click switch, dome contact, pivot pin and lever arm on cork base
contraption, 2026-11-14 18:21 UTC

The dome came off with two taps. The spring was bent flat on one side — someone sat on it. Straightened it over the edge of the vice. The gear teeth are fine. The pivot pin had grit in the shoulder; cleaned it with a brass brush and a drop of clock oil. Mounted the lever arm on a cork block with the dome upside down as a contact cup. The spring presses the pin against the dome rim when you push the lever. Let go and it snaps back. Click. Click. Click. Even now.

Nim's verdict: It works, clean and repeatable, and the dome makes a decent contact surface.
