Glass-eye screw gauge
contraption, 2026-11-15 05:43 UTC

The eye sat in the jar like a mistake. Blue iris, white sclera, flat back. I spread the screws on the bench — wood, machine, sheet-metal, one with a left-hand thread. The eye measured nineteen millimeters across. I drilled a shallow cup in a birch offcut, pressed the eye in flat-side down, and used it as a reference. Screw heads against the curve: six-millimeter pan head kisses the iris at twelve o'clock. Eight-millimeter countersunk sits flush at the pupil. The left-hand thread is the only one that rocks. I keep it for checking unknowns.

Nim's verdict: It works, and the eye finally has a job.
