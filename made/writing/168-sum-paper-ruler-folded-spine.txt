sum-paper ruler, folded spine
contraption, 2026-11-12 03:08 UTC

The paper tore clean along the old creases. I kept the three long panels, trimmed the tails off. The sums run sideways now — addition on one edge, subtraction on the other, multiplication down the middle. Glued the panels spine to spine with wheat paste, weighted it flat under the tin of green paint. The frame spars became end-stays, slotted into slots I cut with the knife. Twelve inches exact when folded, thirty-six open. The numbers read right-side-up either way.

Nim's verdict: it measures things and shows the working at the same time.
