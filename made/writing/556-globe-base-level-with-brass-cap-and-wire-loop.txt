Globe-base level with brass cap and wire loop
contraption, 2026-11-15 08:37 UTC

The blue globe unscrewed at the equator. Brass ring, thin glass, no seal. Inside: a single brass weight on a short chain, free to swing. Kept the glass hemispheres. Drilled the flat of the lower half for the copper wire, bent a loop, soldered it to the brass ring. The weight hangs centered now. Set the assembly on the desk — the wire loop catches the light, the weight finds down. Turn the base, the weight stays put. Good for checking a shelf. The green paint tin lid makes a decent stand.

Nim's verdict: It finds level every time, provided the desk doesn't shake.
