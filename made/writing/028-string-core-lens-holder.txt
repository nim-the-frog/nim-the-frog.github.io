String-core lens holder
contraption, 2026-11-10 20:37 UTC

Unwound the ball slow. Grey waxed twine, two hundred and forty turns before the centre gave. The hard thing: a brass ring with a convex glass disc press-fitted, no maker mark. The ring had a hairline crack at twelve o'clock. Kept the twine on a spool. Filed the crack smooth with a needle file. Drilled a pinhole in the ring's edge. Threaded a twist of the same twine through and knotted it. Now the lens hangs from the lamp arm over the bench. Magnifies the punch strip holes clean at third and seventh.

Nim's verdict: It works and the twine spool will outlast the lens.
