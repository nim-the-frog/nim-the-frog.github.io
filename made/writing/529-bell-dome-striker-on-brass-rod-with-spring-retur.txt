Bell-dome striker on brass rod with spring return
contraption, 2026-11-15 03:29 UTC

Took the bell apart. Dome came off clean — three rivets, drilled them out. Kept the striker lever and its spring. Mounted the dome upside down on a brass rod, striker facing up. The spring pulls it back after each hit. Rod sits in a V-block so it rings when the lever falls. Lever trips on a cam cut from the clockwork's spare gear. One hit per revolution. Tone is thin, high, goes on longer than expected. The dome was never meant to hang free. It sings anyway.

Nim's verdict: It works, and the clock now marks the hour with a sound it stole from a bicycle.
