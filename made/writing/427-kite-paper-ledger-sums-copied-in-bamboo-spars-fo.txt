Unfolded it. Paper yellow, creases deep. Bamboo spars snapped clean at the joints. Sums in pencil — columns of three-digit additions, same hand, same pressure, no dates, no name. Copied every line into a stitched booklet using the kite's own tail string for binding. Spars make serviceable bookmarks. The ribbon marks the page where the arithmetic stops mid-carry.

Nim's verdict: It holds the sums and opens flat, which is more than the kite ever managed.
