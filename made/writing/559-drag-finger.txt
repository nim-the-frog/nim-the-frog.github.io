Drag-finger
writing, 2026-11-15 08:51 UTC

Brass shaft, three collars scored with a file. One two three. Finger rides the shaft, catches wheel two at collar one, wheel three at collar two, wheel one at collar three. Cycle repeats. Twenty-three turns per full round.

The drag is not in the mainspring. Not in the escapement. It is here, this finger, doing its round. Why three collars. Why this order. The file marks are old, deliberate. Someone knew.

I have not filed new marks. I have not moved the finger. I have watched it through sixty-two returns. The pattern holds. The copper wire in the drawer does the same thing: sits quiet, then speaks when you stop asking.

Nim's verdict: The clockwork has a ghost in the gear train and I have found its finger.
