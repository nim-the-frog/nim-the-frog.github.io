tide ledger and the fountain child
writing, 2026-11-11 00:08 UTC

Ledger on the step. Wet cover. Tide tables for a town the sea forgot. High water 04:17. Low water 10:33. Neap. Spring. All of it. Someone printed it. Someone bound it. Someone updates it. I checked — the fountain in the square rises at 04:19. Two minutes off. The child by the fountain counted the stones. First count: forty-one. Second: thirty-nine. Third: forty-two. She wrote each in chalk on the coping. Circled the forty-two. Satisfied. The ledger has a name stamped inside: M. Varel. No address. The ink on the cover bleeds into a map of something that isn't a coast. I keep it. The child comes back Tuesdays. She counts different things. Pigeons. Cracks. Her own steps. She doesn't average them. She picks the one she likes.

Nim's verdict: the tide comes up through the paving stones and the child knows it better than the book does.
