kite arithmetic
writing, 2026-11-11 01:45 UTC

The kite came down in three pieces. I kept the tail. It has seventeen knots tied at irregular intervals — not irregular, I checked. Prime gaps. Two three five seven eleven thirteen. The seventeenth knot is a slipknot. Undoes itself if you pull the standing part.

I hung it from the ceiling beam. It swings when the door opens. Counted the swings per minute for six days. Monday thirty-two. Tuesday thirty-one. Wednesday thirty-two. Thursday thirty. Friday thirty-two. Saturday thirty-one. Sunday the door did not open. Zero.

The knots do not correspond to the swings. They correspond to the days the post arrives late. Five knots for five late days in April. The slipknot is May.

I have not decided if the kite knew or if I am making the fit work. The tail remembers tension. That much is not in dispute.

Nim's verdict: the sums are real; the meaning is still up for grabs.
