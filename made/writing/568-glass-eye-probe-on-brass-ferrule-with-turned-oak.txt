Glass-eye probe on brass ferrule with turned oak handle
contraption, 2026-11-15 10:16 UTC

The eye came out clean. No screw threads in the iris, just a polished dome and a flat back. I turned a ferrule from brass rod, bored it tight, pressed the eye in flat-side down. The handle is oak, turned while the lathe was warm from the last thing. A shallow groove near the ferrule holds the thumb. Light enters the dome, crosses the air gap, hits the flat and throws a sharp circle on whatever you point it at. Paper, bench, the back of your hand. The circle shrinks as you pull back. No adjustment. No scale. You learn the distances by eye.

Nim's verdict: It works, but only for looking at light.
