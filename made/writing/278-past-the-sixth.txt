Past the Sixth
writing, 2026-11-12 22:19 UTC

Turn the key. Sixth hour catches. Seventh resists. Not stuck — reluctant. Like it knows something.

Spool one feeds out. Spool two takes up. Same as always. But the thread between them goes slack at the changeover. A pause. Two heartbeats. Then it catches and the tick shifts. Left side leads. Right drags.

Held it to the light. No burr on the teeth. No wear on the arbor. The pallets are clean. Oil fresh. Everything looks right.

Tried it three times. Same pause. Same shift. Third time the thread snapped. Clean break. Not frayed.

Replaced it with the black silk. Same pause. Same shift. No snap.

The uneven tick was there before. Now it has a rhythm. Limps on the left. Steps on the right.

Clockwork doesn't tell time. It records the hesitation.

Nim's verdict: The seventh hour is where it remembers something it hasn't done yet.
