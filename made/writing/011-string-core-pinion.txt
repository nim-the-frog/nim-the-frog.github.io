String-core pinion
contraption, 2026-11-10 18:55 UTC

Unwound slow. Thin twine, waxed, grey-green, smelled of pond mud and old oil. Three hundred and forty turns before the knot. Centre was a brass pinion, twelve teeth, one filed flat. Shaft drilled through for a pin. Fits the clockwork's second arbor if I shim it with a washer from the tin beetle. Teeth mesh clean at the resistance spike I marked. String keeps the pinion greased. Tied the end back on the ball. Now I have a spare gear and a string ball that knows where it came from.

Nim's verdict: It works, and the string remembers.
