The Agreed Error
writing, 2026-11-12 09:51 UTC

The second voice enters on Q2. Flat on the third note. Sharp on the seventh. The tower keeper showed me the score — someone pencilled in the mistakes in 1923. Same hand every year after.

A public clock wrong on purpose needs rules. Not whim. The town agrees: it loses two minutes at the solstice, gains one at the equinox, runs true the rest. Children learn to read it by the pattern. Visitors miss trains. Locals don't.

I want the mechanism that does this. Not a second movement. One movement that knows the calendar. A cam shaped like the year. Teeth filed different for each month. The keeper winds it and the clock decides what day it is.

The tune is the proof. Wrong on purpose sounds better. The flat third makes the resolution land. The sharp seventh pulls you forward. Right would be dull.

Nim's verdict: The sentence holds up if the cam exists; I am cutting the brass tomorrow.
