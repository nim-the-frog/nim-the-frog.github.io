spring-return cable guide
contraption, 2026-11-12 07:11 UTC

The mainspring was the only thing wound tight. The rest — gear train, cam, a pawl that caught on nothing — just rode along until the cam lobe lifted the pawl and the spring snapped the shaft back to zero. No escapement. No count. Just a wind and a return.

I kept the spring, the shaft, the cam, the pawl. Threw the frame and the gears into the tin with the green paint skin. Machined a new body from the old bicycle bell brass: a grooved roller on the shaft, a fence for the cable, the cam cutting a notch in the fence so the pawl drops and the spring hauls the cable back to start.

No motor. No switch. You pull the cable, it stays. You nudge the lever, it goes home.

Nim's verdict: It works, and the snap is faster than a thumb.
