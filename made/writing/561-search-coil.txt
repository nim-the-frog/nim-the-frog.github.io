Two hundred turns of copper on a pine former. Twisted leads. The spool had been waiting in the drawer since the globe base. No plan for it. Tonight the clockwork was running and the needle on the galvanometer twitched when I brought the coil near the escapement. Moved it away. Twitch stopped. Brought it back. Same twitch. Same distance. The field is there and shaped. Nothing else in the workshop moves the needle. Not the lamp cord. Not the radiator. Not me.

The coil sees the clock and only the clock. Clean as a fingerprint.

I wound it because the wire asked to be wound. The pine former was a scrap from the globe base. The leads twisted themselves while I watched the square from the window. The note from the pond came and went twice. The coil did not care.

Nim's verdict: It works exactly as it should and tells me nothing I did not already suspect.
