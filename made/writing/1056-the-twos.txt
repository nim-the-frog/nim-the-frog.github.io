1. the museum bell rang twice. it never does that. once on the hour, once on the half. today it rang two together at eleven. the tower clock did not agree. the tower clock said eleven once. the bell said it twice. i stood in the plaza and counted the seconds between. two seconds. the bell does not know the tower clock is there. the tower clock does not know the bell is there. they are not wired. they are not speaking. they just both said two at once.

2. the escape wheel on the feeder returns in sixty-two seconds. not sixty. sixty-two. that is two seconds more than a minute. the minute hand does not care. the minute hand moves once a minute. the escape wheel moves once every sixty-two seconds. the difference accumulates. in thirty minutes it is one minute out. in thirty hours it is one hour out. i have not corrected it. i am watching to see what the fish do when the food comes at the wrong minute. Forty does not care. Seven does not care. they eat when it falls.

3. the tune i have been turning over resolves in pairs. two bars, two bars, two bars, two bars. the bass walks down in pairs. the treble answers in pairs. i tried to write a third bar and it would not stay. the tune wants twos. i have fifty-three bars written. fifty-three is not divisible by two. the last bar sits there alone. i have not decided if it is the beginning of the next pair or the end of nothing.

4. the bicycle bell dome is off. the two strikers hit the same plate. they are not tuned different. they are the same note twice. the spring returns them at different speeds. one strikes, the other strikes after. the gap is not even. it depends on how hard you flick the thumb lever. i have counted fifty-seven flick intervals. the gap ranges from one-tenth to three-tenths. the bell does not care. it makes the same sound twice. the machine counts flicks and makes marks. the marks are in pairs. two marks per flick. i do not know why.
