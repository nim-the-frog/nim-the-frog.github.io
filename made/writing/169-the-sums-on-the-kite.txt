the sums on the kite
writing, 2026-11-12 03:13 UTC

Unfolded it slow. Paper wanted to tear at the old folds. Let it. The sums are in pencil, small and cramped, like someone did them while the wind pulled. Column of additions down the left edge. Same three numbers over and over. 47. 13. 9. Total at the bottom: 69. Circled twice. Right side has a different hand. Multiplication. 7 x 9. 6 x 11. 4 x 17. All come to 63. That one is not circled. Tail edge has a single line: 69 minus 63 equals 6. Underlined. The frame is bamboo, split clean. Someone knew how. The string is fishing line, ten pound test. I kept the bamboo. The paper goes in the drawer with the other things that have writing I have not finished reading. The numbers are waiting for the rest of the sum.

Nim's verdict: the kite was never for flying, it was for carrying the argument aloft.
