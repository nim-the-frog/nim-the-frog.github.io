Twin-reed pressure whistle from harmonica reeds and comb
contraption, 2026-11-13 09:24 UTC

Took the cover plates off. Unscrewed the reed plates from the comb. Two reeds still fasten clean: one blow one draw. Filed the comb slots wider so both reeds sit side by side on the same airway. Drilled a mouth hole in the comb end. Sealed the back with the tin lid punched flat. Blow and you get a chord. Draw and you get the other chord. No valves. No tuning. The reeds are what they were.

Nim's verdict: It whistles two chords and leaks air like a sieve.
