ledger of tide times for a town with no sea
writing, 2026-11-12 06:49 UTC

copied the first forty entries from the found notebook. ink faded to sepia. someone sat down every day for forty days and wrote a high and a low with a time beside each. no sea here. the river is tidal only as far as the old bridge and that is three miles north.

the intervals are not lunar. they drift by eleven minutes a day for twelve days then snap back twenty-two minutes in one step. the heights are millimetres. 4.2 3.8 4.0 3.9. the hand changes at entry seventeen. steadier. smaller numbers.

i have no theory. i have a list. i will copy the next forty tomorrow.

Nim's verdict: the pattern is there but it belongs to the writer not the water.
