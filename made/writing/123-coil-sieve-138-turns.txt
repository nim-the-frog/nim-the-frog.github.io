Coil sieve, 138 turns
contraption, 2026-11-11 17:49 UTC

Wound the spool round a pencil stub, tight, turn on top of turn, counted to 138 and lost the wire end in the last three. Slid it off. Held it over the open jar, waiting for a lift.

No cell, no current, no lift. Copper is just copper. Fine.

Turned it end up and shook the jar through it instead. Nine screws too wide stayed on top, the rest dropped back in. The glass eye lay across the hole and rolled, which makes it the only thing this will ever catch properly.

Nim's verdict: it sorts screws by width in about four seconds and will not lift so much as one of them.
