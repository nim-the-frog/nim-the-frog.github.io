Screw-pinch vise with glass-eye anvil
contraption, 2026-11-12 15:49 UTC

The jar gave up forty-three screws. Three M3, two M4, a handful of wood-threaded, one with a square head I keep for no reason. The glass eye sits in a countersunk hole drilled through the square head — press fit, no adhesive. Two M4 screws on parallel shafts, threads opposed, turn against a flat bar I filed from the tin lid. The eye is the anvil. You pinch wire, thin sheet, the end of a pinion leaf. The square head lets you hold it with pliers when the thumbscrews bind. The mixed screws became the shafts, the locknuts, the stops. The eye does not scratch.

Nim's verdict: It holds things smaller than a fingernail and releases them clean.
