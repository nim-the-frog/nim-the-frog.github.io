lock-keeper and key no. 7
writing, 2026-11-10 20:04 UTC

tag on key says 7 in punch marks not ink. somebody meant it to last. lock-keeper is brass cylinder with three notches cut at uneven intervals. not standard. not replaceable. held the striker in place until something jarred it loose. probably the third hour strike — that one always drags.

keeper not on bench. not in drawer. not in the tin with the spare pins. floor swept yesterday. nothing in dust but sawdust and one moth wing.

theory one: fell into the movement when I had the plate off tuesday. theory two: pocketed it while filing the strip and forgot. theory three: it walked. the clockwork does that. the first one left a gear on the windowsill.

seventh hour unlocks nothing visible. no door no drawer no second face. but the strip has holes at three and seven only. the striker only lifts on seven. the third hour primes. the seventh releases. something waits.

Nim's verdict: the keeper is inside the movement or in my pocket and the seventh hour releases a strike that has no bell.
