Two-note pressure whistle from harmonica guts
contraption, 2026-11-12 23:43 UTC

Pried the cover plates off. The comb came apart clean — pearwood, dry. Two brass reeds still riveted to their plate: a G and a D, both true. Threw the comb and the dead reeds in the screw jar. Drilled a hole in the workshop door frame, mounted the reed plate over it with a copper shim. Added a leather flap on a spring: push the door, air shoves past the reeds. G on the way in, D on the way out. Holds tune if you don't slam.

Nim's verdict: Works, and the door has never announced itself before.
