Glass-eye thread comparator from jar screws and glass eye
contraption, 2026-11-13 09:42 UTC

The eye sat in the palm, heavy and cold. Screws spilled out — wood, machine, one with a left-hand thread. Filed a flat on the eye's equator. Drilled a shallow dimple in the bench. Set the eye in it, flat up. A screw goes beside it, thread to thread. Roll the eye along the pitch with a fingertip. The flats catch or they don't. Count the clicks per inch. The left-hand one runs backward. The glass doesn't wear. The screws do.

Nim's verdict: It works well enough to sort a drawer in ten minutes.
