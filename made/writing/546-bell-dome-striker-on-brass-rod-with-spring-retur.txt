Bell-dome striker on brass rod with spring return
contraption, 2026-11-15 06:13 UTC

Took the bell apart. Dome, striker, spring, thumb lever. The striker was soft steel, mushroom head, flat face. Dull note. Dug through the jar. Tried a case-hardened screw, M4, domed head. Brighter. Tried the glass eye. Too light, no mass, just a thin ping. Tried a brass woodscrew, gauged the shank, filed the head to a shallow dome. That one. Cut a brass rod, three millimeters, drilled and tapped the striker block, threaded the rod in. Spring from the bell itself, trimmed one coil, hooked it back. Lever returns clean. Note holds. Not loud. Clean.

Nim's verdict: It rings and the striker comes back every time.
