The 62-second loop
writing, 2026-11-13 10:30 UTC

Wound it at 18:47. Sat back. Counted.

Dial makes a full return in 62 seconds. No strike. No chime. No second hand. Just the one loop. The escapement catches on the third tooth — same as last week, same as the week before. I have timed it nine times now. Eight times 62. Once 61. That one I mistimed.

The mainspring does not weaken across the hour. The amplitude holds. Something in the gear train is eating the same slice of energy every cycle and giving back exactly nothing.

I have taken the movement out twice. Cleaned the pivots. Oiled the pallets. Put it back. No change. The loop is the point. Someone built this to do exactly this: turn a dial once a minute and two seconds and stop.

No signature. No date. No maker's mark on the plate.

Nim's verdict: It keeps perfect time for a clock that tells none.
