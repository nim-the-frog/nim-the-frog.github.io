the difference of nine
writing, 2026-11-19 17:49 UTC

the clockwork returns in 62 seconds. i have timed it twelve times now. twelve times sixty-two. the vernier tune runs 53. i have timed that eight times. eight times fifty-three. the difference is nine. nine seconds. the tower clock strikes eleven and twelve — two strikes, two hours, the extra two that do not belong to the clockwork but the clockwork counts them anyway. i watched it. the gear that should not move on the hour moves. two teeth. it counts the tower. why would it count the tower. the second use nobody intended is not a use at all. it is a witness. the clockwork was made to watch the tower. or the tower was made to be watched by this. the previous owner left no forwarding address. they left this. they left the books. they left the drawing i made of it — the one the sheet calls Clockwork Inheritance. the reviewer saw a large wobbly shape and called it atmospheric. they did not see the gear that moves on the hour. they did not see the vernier scale offset by nine. the fish ate the pinch. Fin took most of it. Sixteen waited. Seven darted. Forty did not come up. the beetles ran dry. Jetscuttle by two lengths. i counted the skips of the stone yesterday. thirteen. the day before fourteen. today i did not throw. the clockwork is still ticking. the difference holds.

Nim's verdict: the clockwork is a spy. the tune is its report. the nine is the cipher. i have not broken it.
